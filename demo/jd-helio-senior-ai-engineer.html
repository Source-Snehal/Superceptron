Helio Analytics is a Series B B2B SaaS company (~130 people) that helps mid-market retail and e-commerce businesses understand their data. We're embedding AI into the core product — starting with a natural-language query layer that lets non-technical users ask questions of their data in plain English. This role leads that build.

The role

You'll own the design, build, and productionisation of Helio's LLM-powered features. The immediate focus is our natural-language analytics interface — an NL-to-SQL layer with semantic search, context-aware follow-up, and automated insight generation. You'll work closely with our product and data platform teams and report into the VP of Engineering.

This is not a research role. We need someone who has shipped LLM features to real users, knows what breaks in production, and can make sound architectural decisions under commercial constraints.

What you'll own

  • Design and build the NL-to-SQL pipeline end-to-end — prompt architecture, retrieval, query validation, and the feedback loop
  • Build and maintain the RAG layer that grounds queries in each customer's schema and business context
  • Own the LLM evaluation framework — define metrics, build evals, track regressions
  • Work with the data platform team on the vector store and embedding pipeline (we're on AWS, moving toward pgvector)
  • Contribute to our model selection and fine-tuning strategy as the product matures
  • Participate in architecture decisions across the wider engineering team

What we're looking for

Essential

  • 3+ years working on production ML or AI systems (not just research or prototypes)
  • Hands-on experience building with LLMs — prompt engineering, RAG, function calling, evaluation
  • Strong Python and API engineering (FastAPI or equivalent)
  • Experience with vector databases (pgvector, Pinecone, Weaviate, or similar)
  • Comfortable working in AWS (Lambda, ECS, RDS, S3)
  • Can own a system end-to-end, from design through to on-call

Nice to have

  • Experience with NL-to-SQL or text-to-code systems specifically
  • Fine-tuning experience (LoRA, RLHF, DPO)
  • Background in analytics, BI, or data platform products
  • Experience working at Series A–C SaaS companies
  • Familiarity with LangChain, LlamaIndex, or DSPy
