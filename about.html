About us

We're the team that makes AI work for your agency.

We're not a platform. We're a small, founder-led team focused on one thing: AI CV screening for independent recruiting firms. The person you email is the person who does the work.

Our story

Born from frustration with manual work that didn't need to exist.

Superceptron was founded with a specific focus: CV screening for independent recruiting firms. Not because it was the easiest problem to solve, but because it's the one that wastes the most consultant time and is most directly automatable.

The firms we work with already have an ATS and a CRM they know well. They don't want to rip them out. They don't want a platform with a six-month implementation. They want someone to take the CV pile off their desk and give them back a ranked shortlist — and they want to talk to the person doing it.

That's what we do. One focused service, done well, with a direct line to the founder.
