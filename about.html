About us

We read resumes the way a hiring manager would.

Most tools match your job ad against a resume and score the overlap. That's not reading, and it's why good people get filtered out for describing the same work in different words.

How we work

Built by someone who's made the call from the hiring side.

Superceptron is built by a senior data scientist, not a recruiter. That's deliberate. For data, ML and engineering roles, the useful judgement isn't "does this resume match the ad" — it's "could this person actually do the job." That's a call I've made from the hiring side, many times.

Every resume gets read in full. A ranked shortlist comes back with the reasoning written out for each name, including who missed and why. Nothing gets rejected by a machine, and the final call is always yours.

Small and founder-led, deliberately. You're not going to get passed to an account manager.
