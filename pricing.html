Common questions

What counts as one role?

One job description screened against one batch of resumes. If you're running a DevOps search and a product manager search simultaneously, that's two roles.

What if I have more than 50 resumes for a role?

Get in touch — we'll quote per batch. Larger volumes don't change the process, just the number of resumes we're screening.

Do I need to change my ATS or CRM?

No. You email us resumes and a job spec. The shortlist comes back by email. We work alongside Bullhorn, Crelate, JobDiva — whatever you already use.

How does the two-stage process work?

First, AI scores and ranks every resume against your job spec — reading for meaning, not keyword matches, with a written rationale for each candidate. Then a person reviews the shortlist before it reaches you, to catch edge cases and calibrate to what your clients actually want. You see the reasoning behind every ranking. You make the final call.
