What we do

One offer.

Boutique tech recruiting firms lose hours every week to resume screening — reading 80 applications to build a shortlist of six. We take that work off your desk. Send us a batch and a job spec, and we send back a ranked shortlist with a written assessment of every candidate — within the hour.

How it works

AI scoring. Human review.
Every shortlist.

Engine 1 — AI

Scores and ranks every resume against your job spec

The moment your batch lands, AI reads every resume for meaning — not keyword matches. Within minutes, every candidate is ranked with a written rationale: why they fit, what to probe on a call, any gaps worth noting. You see the reasoning behind every ranking, not just a score.

→ Reads for semantic fit, not keyword density. A resume that says "built ML pipelines in PyTorch" matches a spec asking for "Python ML experience" — a keyword tool misses that entirely.
Engine 2 — Human review

Reviews every shortlist before it reaches you

Before any shortlist leaves our desk, a person goes through it — not to re-screen from scratch, but to catch what AI tends to miss: strong candidates with unconventional career paths or non-standard resume formats. We also calibrate to what your specific clients actually want, and that calibration compounds over time.

→ Results sharpen for each client as we learn what they value. That's not something a self-serve tool can replicate.

You always see the reasoning behind every shortlist. You always make the final call — we just get you there faster.

vs. the alternatives

The honest comparison.

Most recruiters at small firms land on the same three options before deciding. Here's where each one falls short.

Alternative 01 ChatGPT & off-the-shelf AI tools
✕

You do all the work — writing prompts, handling file uploads, deciding what to evaluate. No integration into your actual workflow. No accountability if the shortlist misses someone good.

✓

Screening plugs directly into your existing process. Send an email, get a shortlist back. A real person reviews and stands behind every output — not a black box you have to babysit.

Alternative 02 Self-serve screening softwareShortlistHQ and similar tools
✕

You still do the work. Log in, upload resumes, configure the model, interpret the results — every search. No human reviews the output. Edge cases and formatting quirks are your problem.

✓

Our approach requires zero tool adoption. Send resumes by email — we handle the rest, including the human review step that self-serve tools skip entirely.

Alternative 03 Hiring an internal ops or admin person
✕

Fixed salary cost whether you have 80 resumes to screen or eight. Months of ramp-up and training. Ongoing management overhead that compounds as your team grows.

✓

Pay only for the volume that exists right now. No recruiting, no onboarding, no performance reviews. Flex up when a big mandate lands — step back when it's quiet.
